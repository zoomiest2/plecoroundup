Any mix of formats
What can I paste?
One phrase per line, fields in any order, split by |, a tab, - or =. Or no separator at all: 谢谢 xièxie thank you.
Blocks of 2–3 lines (characters, pinyin, English) with a blank line between phrases.
Only English, or only characters? Add it anyway. Pinyin is filled from the characters automatically, and Fill with Claude translates whatever is still missing.

Importing on your phone
Save the file to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android). In Pleco: Flashcards → Import / Export → Import Cards, pick the file, keep format as plain text, then tap Import.
Each line is characters⇥pinyin⇥English, and the // line puts the cards in your category.
